Financial Safety

Protect your money and personal information.

🔐

Never Share OTP

Never share your OTP with anyone, even if they claim to be from a bank.

📱

Protect UPI PIN

Keep your UPI PIN private and never share it with anyone.

☎️

Beware of Fake Calls

Do not trust unknown callers asking for bank or payment information.

🔗

Avoid Suspicious Links

Do not click unknown links received through messages or emails.

🔑

Use Strong Passwords

Use strong passwords and avoid sharing them with others.

🚨

Report Fraud

If you notice suspicious activity, report it immediately.

About MoneySmart Rural

Financial literacy for a better future.

Our Project

MoneySmart Rural is a financial literacy awareness website designed especially for rural students.

The website provides simple and practical information about saving, budgeting, banking, digital payments, loans and financial safety.

Project Objectives

  • Improve basic financial awareness.
  • Encourage regular saving habits.
  • Teach simple budgeting techniques.
  • Promote safe digital payments.
  • Create awareness about online financial fraud.
  • Encourage responsible financial decisions.
