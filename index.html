About MoneySmart Rural

MoneySmart Rural is a financial literacy website designed to help rural students understand basic financial concepts in a simple and practical way.

The website focuses on saving, budgeting, banking, digital payments, loans, interest and financial safety.

Our Objectives

  • Improve basic financial awareness.
  • Encourage regular saving habits.
  • Teach simple budgeting skills.
  • Promote safe digital payments.
  • Create awareness about online financial fraud.
