🛡️ Digital Money Safety

Follow these rules to protect your money.

🔐 Never share your OTP with anyone.
🔑 Never share your UPI PIN.
📱 Don't click unknown payment links.
⚠️ Be careful of fake calls and messages.
🏦 Contact your bank if you notice suspicious transactions.
🚨 If something seems suspicious, stop the transaction and verify first.

ℹ️ About Our Project

This website is created as part of our college project on Financial Literacy for Rural Students.

The objective of this project is to help students understand saving, budgeting, banking, digital payments and financial safety.

Our goal is to make financial knowledge simple, useful and accessible.
